How the score works

Effective contributors (EFF) = 1 / Σ(commit share²) over the recent window, bots excluded. If one person makes 90% of the commits, EFF is about 1.2 — essentially a one-person project.

  • Scarcity 1 / (1 + (EFF − 1) / 2.5) multiplies everything, so many contributors means a low score no matter how many stars.
  • Base 100 × scarcity × (0.4 + 0.4 × popularity + 0.2 × demand), where popularity and demand are smoothly saturating functions of stars and issues per effective contributor.
  • Outsider PR acceptance is shown for information and only matters when it is zero: if no outsider PR was merged the score is cut (or the repo is dropped when there were 5 or more).
  • Crowding: more than 5 outsider PR authors in the recent window lowers the score (25 authors halve it), because many people are already contributing.
  • Entry issues (help wanted + good first issue): 5 or more gives a +10% bonus. Research projects get a small +5%.
  • Excluded: dead repos, docs / guides / example collections, company-run repos, repos closed to outsiders, PR backlogs (more than 50 open PRs and more open than merged in the window), and repos with more than 1,000 all-time contributors.

These are heuristics and can be wrong. Treat the ranking as a starting point, not a verdict.
